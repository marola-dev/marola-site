## Participatory Ecomonitoring Platform

Everything we generate about the Lagoon Complex — the monthly boat collections, the Exhibition data, the information history and the compilation of studies already done on the lagoon — gathered in a cloud platform, open and continually updated.

One place for researchers, students and managers to follow the health of the lagoon, with an artificial intelligence assistant that answers questions from that data.

marola's map follows that platform with the **Lagoon Complex (CL)** area: the lagoon's beaches and sampling points, the surrounding trails and the water quality published by IMA/SC, side by side with the data from the [Instituto Canto das Águas](https://cantodasaguas.org/).
