## Partners

marola is built in the open, as citizen science, and it does not go alone. These are the partners that agreed to be listed here.

## ECO Lab @UFSC

The [ECO Lab](https://ondaseventos.ufsc.br/) is the laboratory at the Federal University of Santa Catarina, in Florianópolis, that runs **Ondas & Ventos**, the university's waves-and-winds site. The partnership brings marola alongside the lab's work with the sea and the wind on the Santa Catarina coast, in open data and citizen science.

## Canto das Águas

![Instituto Canto das Águas](/vendor/canto-das-aguas/logo.png 600x203)

The [Instituto Canto das Águas](https://cantodasaguas.org/) is a centre for innovation in Earth sciences in Imaruí, Santa Catarina, bringing science, education and regenerative production together to restore the Southern Santa Catarina Lagoon Complex. With schools, universities, public managers and the communities of the basin's 12 municipalities, the institute keeps an open-air laboratory and a technical-scientific exhibition about the lagoon.

marola and Canto das Águas meet in ecomonitoring: the institute's boat expeditions and monthly collections generate the data that the [Participatory Ecomonitoring Platform](ecomonitoramento.html) gathers, and marola takes the same question to the map — how healthy is the water, and where is it safe to go in. The partnership is open: the data, the studies and the code stay public, so anyone can check them, cite them and continue the work.

## Leave No Trace

![Leave No Trace, Proud Partner](/vendor/lnt/proud-partner.png 1717x728)

The [Leave No Trace Center for Outdoor Ethics](https://lnt.org/) teaches people to enjoy nature without leaving a trace, with seven simple principles for anyone outdoors. marola is an official Proud Partner: whoever goes to the sea should leave it as they found it.

The seven principles:

- Plan Ahead and Prepare
- Travel and Camp on Durable Surfaces
- Dispose of Waste Properly
- Leave What You Find
- Minimize Campfire Impacts
- Respect Wildlife
- Be Considerate of Other Visitors

Leave No Trace Seven Principles © 1999 by the Leave No Trace Center for Outdoor Ethics: [www.LNT.org](https://lnt.org/).

## OpenDiving

![OpenDiving](/vendor/opendiving/icon.svg 24x24)

[OpenDiving](https://opendiving.app) is an open-source dive logbook ([code on GitHub](https://github.com/opendiving/opendiving)). marola is on the project's list of partner apps, and the two link to each other: marola says where and when the water is good, OpenDiving keeps the dive.

## Ouroboric

![Ouroboric](/vendor/ouroboric/ouroboric-icon-dark.svg 100x100)

[Ouroboric](https://ouroboric.dev) ([code on GitHub](https://github.com/ouroboric-dev)) is Bruno Valerio's ([@brunogbv](https://github.com/brunogbv), [valerio.dev](https://valerio.dev)) open factory: it builds Yggdrasil, which builds software with agents while people steer the direction, and every run makes the factory measurably better — in the open, like marola.

## piabrota

![piabrota](/vendor/piabrota/favicon.svg 100x100)

[piabrota](https://piabrota.art) is a group of open-source projects made in Rio de Janeiro, each in its own repository with its own licence and tests. marola is one of them.
