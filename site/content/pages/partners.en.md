## Partners

marola is built in the open, as citizen science, and it does not go alone. These are the partners that agreed to be listed here.

## Leave No Trace

![Leave No Trace, Proud Partner](/vendor/lnt/proud-partner.png 1717x728)

The [Leave No Trace Center for Outdoor Ethics](https://lnt.org/) teaches people to enjoy nature without leaving a trace, with seven simple principles for anyone outdoors. marola is an official Proud Partner: whoever goes to the sea should leave it as they found it.

The seven principles:

- Plan Ahead and Prepare
- Travel and Camp on Durable Surfaces
- Dispose of Waste Properly
- Leave What You Find
- Minimize Campfire Impacts
- Respect Wildlife
- Be Considerate of Other Visitors

Leave No Trace Seven Principles © 1999 by the Leave No Trace Center for Outdoor Ethics: [www.LNT.org](https://lnt.org/).

## OpenDiving

![OpenDiving](/vendor/opendiving/icon.svg 24x24)

[OpenDiving](https://opendiving.app) is an open-source dive logbook ([code on GitHub](https://github.com/opendiving/opendiving)). marola is on the project's list of partner apps, and the two link to each other: marola says where and when the water is good, OpenDiving keeps the dive.

## piabrota

![piabrota](/vendor/piabrota/favicon.svg 100x100)

[piabrota](https://piabrota.art) is a group of open-source projects made in Rio de Janeiro, each in its own repository with its own licence and tests. marola is one of them.
